# Privacy Policy — Berkeley Landmarks

**Last updated:** June 6, 2026

## Overview
Berkeley Landmarks is a mobile app for exploring architectural landmarks and walking tours in Berkeley, California. This policy describes how the app handles your data.

## Data We Collect

### Location Data
The app requests access to your device location solely to show your position on the map and suggest nearby landmarks. Location data is:
- Used only while the app is active
- Never stored on our servers
- Never shared with third parties

### Photos
The app may request photo library access if you choose to submit landmark photos. Photos are only uploaded with your explicit action and consent.

### No Analytics or Tracking
We do not collect analytics, use tracking SDKs, or share any user data with advertisers or third parties.

## Data Storage
- **Favorites and visited landmarks**: Stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage
- **No server accounts**: The app works fully offline with static data
- **No cloud sync**: Your data stays on your device

## Third-Party Services
The app uses map tiles from Apple Maps (built into iOS). No other third-party services are active in the production app.

## Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes
We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date.

## Contact
For questions about this policy, contact: berkeleylandmarks@manus.space
