# Privacy Policy — Berkeley Tours

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026

## Overview
Berkeley Tours is a guide to the landmarks, architects and walks of Berkeley, California. Almost all of it works with no account, and what you do in it stays on your phone.

## What stays on your phone
- **Your copy of the guide**: the places you've visited, the walks you've walked, the pages you've turned down and the places you've asked the guide to watch are kept on your phone and nowhere else.
- **The maps** are part of the app and work offline.

## Location
The guide asks for your location to show where you are on the map, to collect each stop of a walk as you reach it, and to stamp the places you visit. If you ask it to watch a place, it uses your location in the background to tell you when you're near — only if you allow "Always" location. Your location is used on your phone only: it is never sent to our server or shared with anyone.

## Accounts (optional)
You need an account only to send photographs, corrections or proposals to the guide's editors. To sign in, you give your email address and enter a six-figure code we email to you; there is no password. We keep:
- your email address, and a name taken from it;
- when you joined and when you last signed in.

The sign-in code is delivered by our email provider, Resend, which receives your address for that purpose only.

## What you send the editors
- **Photographs, corrections and proposals** are stored on our server and read by the editors before anything goes into the guide. Approved ones appear in the guide without your name.
- The text of a correction (not who sent it) may be checked first by an automated AI reviewer, to flag spam or obvious mistakes for the editors.
- **Photo Library**: the guide asks for access only when you choose photographs to send, and sends only the ones you pick.

## Deleting your account
In the Appendix, under "Your Guide", choose **Delete your account**. This deletes your account and email address from our server, along with every photograph, correction and proposal of yours the editors haven't yet approved. What they have approved stays in the guide, with no name on it. Your stamps and visits stay on your phone until you delete the app.

## No analytics, advertising or tracking
We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs, don't track you across apps or websites, and don't sell or share your data.

## Children
The guide is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
