## intro

Walk 05, West Berkeley. Nine stops, about a mile and a half, and an hour and a half.

This is the oldest part of Berkeley. The Ohlone lived at the mouth of Strawberry Creek for thousands of years, and the settlement called Ocean View grew up here long before the university arrived. You'll pass workers' cottages, immigrant halls and two of the oldest churches in town. Walter Ratcliff will speak at the last stop, his Day Nursery. His words were written for this guide from the historical record, and his voice is a dramatization. At the other stops, you'll hear me.

We begin at Fox Court, on University Avenue.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 05, at the Day Nursery on Sixth Street. University Avenue is a few blocks north. If you'd like to see the factories that once filled this side of town, Walk 11 covers West Berkeley's industry.
