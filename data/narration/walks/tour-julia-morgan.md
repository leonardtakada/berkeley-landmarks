## intro

Walk 09, the Julia Morgan Legacy. Six stops, about a mile and a half, and an hour.

Julia Morgan was the first woman licensed to practice architecture in California, trained in Berkeley and schooled in Paris. This walk takes you from her early church on College Avenue to the City Club, the building many call her masterpiece. She'll tell you about each one herself, and at the women's gymnasium Bernard Maybeck, her first teacher, joins her. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations.

We begin at the Julia Morgan Center for the Arts, on College Avenue.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 09, at the Berkeley City Club on Durant Avenue. Downtown is a few blocks west, and the campus a block north.
