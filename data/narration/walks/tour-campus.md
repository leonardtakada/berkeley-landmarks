## intro

Walk 04, the University of California campus. Ten stops, about a mile and a quarter, and an hour and a half.

Most of what you'll see was built to the Phoebe Hearst plan for the campus, and most of it by one man, John Galen Howard, the university's supervising architect. He'll tell you about his buildings himself, and Bernard Maybeck will speak at the Faculty Club. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. At the other stops, you'll hear me.

We begin at Sather Gate, on the bridge over Strawberry Creek.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 04, at Founders Rock, where the town and the university got their name. Hearst Avenue is just behind you, and Walk 03 begins a little way west, on Spruce Street.
