## intro

Walk 02, South of Campus. Seven stops, about a mile and a half, and an hour.

A century ago this was a neighborhood of professors, clubs and churches, before the university grew over most of it. What survives includes some of the best buildings in Berkeley. Three of the architects will tell you about their own work: Walter Ratcliff, Bernard Maybeck and Julia Morgan. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. At the other stops, you'll hear me.

We begin at the Anna Head School, at Channing Way and Bowditch Street.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 02. You're on Piedmont Avenue, at the edge of the campus. Walk 12 begins a block away on Piedmont Avenue, if you'd like to keep going.
