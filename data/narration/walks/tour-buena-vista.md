## intro

Walk 10, Buena Vista and the Hilltop. Seven stops, about a mile, and an hour, nearly all of it uphill.

You'll start beside the north fork of Strawberry Creek, among some of Berkeley's earliest and grandest houses, and climb past mid-century modern houses to the open-air Temple of Wings and the castle at the top. Ernest Coxhead, Bernard Maybeck and John Hudson Thomas will tell you about their own buildings. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. At the other stops, you'll hear me.

We begin at Allanoke, at Le Roy Avenue and Ridge Road.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 10, at the top of Buena Vista Way. Take a moment for the view before you head down.
