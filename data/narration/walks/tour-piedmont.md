## intro

Walk 12, the Piedmont Avenue Stroll. Seven stops, about a mile, and an hour.

You'll walk the boulevard Frederick Law Olmsted laid out beside the College of California, past fraternity row and the Greenes' Thorsen House, to John Galen Howard's Memorial Stadium, and up the hill to Bowles Hall. William Hays and John Galen Howard will tell you about their own work. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. At the other stops, you'll hear me.

We begin at the Sigma Pi Chapter House, at 2395 Piedmont Avenue.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 12, at Hearst Avenue and Highland Place, on the north side of the campus. Walk 08 passes Charles Keeler's house, just up Highland Place.
