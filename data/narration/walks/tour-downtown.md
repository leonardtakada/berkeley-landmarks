## intro

Walk 01, Downtown Berkeley. Ten stops, about a mile, and an hour and a half if you take your time.

This is the commercial heart of the city: the civic buildings around the park, the banks and the grand hotel on Shattuck Avenue, and the Art Deco library. At three of the stops, the architect will tell you about the building himself: Walter Ratcliff, James Plachek and William Yelland. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. At the other stops, you'll hear me.

We begin at Old City Hall on Martin Luther King Jr. Way, facing the park.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 01. You're at Bancroft Way and Shattuck Avenue, the place where downtown meets the university. The campus is a short walk east along Bancroft.
