## intro

Walk 06, Elmwood and Claremont. Seven stops, a little over two miles, and an hour and a half.

These were streetcar neighborhoods. When the lines reached out here early in the last century, houses, a village shopping street and a grand hotel followed. Julia Morgan, Walter Ratcliff and James Plachek will tell you about their own buildings. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. At the other stops, you'll hear me.

We begin at the William Colby House, at Channing Way and Warring Street.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 06, at the Claremont Hotel. The hotel has a Berkeley address, but strictly speaking it stands just over the line, in Oakland.
