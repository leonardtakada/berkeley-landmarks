## intro

Walk 07, the Historic Landmarks Walk. Twelve stops, nearly three miles, and about two and a half hours.

This is the guide's long walk: a single route through the buildings that define Berkeley, from a Greene and Greene bungalow and Maybeck's great church, through downtown, up to Normandy Village, and on to the rock where the town got its name. Six of the architects will tell you about their own work: Bernard Maybeck, Julia Morgan, James Plachek, William Yelland, Walter Ratcliff and John Galen Howard. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. At the other stops, you'll hear me.

We begin at the Thorsen House, on Piedmont Avenue at Bancroft Way.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 07, at Founders Rock. You've crossed the town from Piedmont Avenue, through downtown, to the Northside. For a closer look at any part of it, each neighborhood has a walk of its own.
