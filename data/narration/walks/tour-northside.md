## intro

Walk 03, Northside and the Hills. Seven stops, two miles, and an hour and a half, much of it uphill.

At every stop on this walk, the architect will tell you about the building: William Yelland, John Galen Howard, Bernard Maybeck and John Hudson Thomas. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. Where a story runs past their lifetimes, I'll add a word at the end.

We begin at Normandy Village, on Spruce Street just above Hearst Avenue.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 03. You're high on Buena Vista Way, in the hills the 1923 fire swept through. Walks 08 and 10 also climb up here, with more of the houses on this hill.
