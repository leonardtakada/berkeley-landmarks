## intro

Walk 11, Industrial Heritage. Six stops, about three and a half miles, and two hours.

Long before the university, West Berkeley was the town's working end. There was a wharf here by 1853, and a grist mill and a lumberyard by 1856. The bay and the railroads brought factories, and immigrants came to work in them. This walk visits the mills and plants that survived: knitting, tanning, pasta, Heinz's "57 Varieties" and the metal frames of the modern shop window. On this walk, you'll hear only me.

We begin at the Cooper Woodworking Building, on Addison Street.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 11, at the old tannery on Fourth Street. The shops and cafés around you now fill buildings that once made leather, ink and machinery. Walk 05 explores the older homes and churches of West Berkeley, a few blocks south.
