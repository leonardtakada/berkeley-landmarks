## intro

Walk 08, the Bernard Maybeck Trail. Fifteen stops, nearly three miles, and about two and a half hours, with a long climb at the end.

This walk follows one architect from his clubhouse and his great church south of the campus, through the university, to Highland Place, where he built his first house for a client, and up into La Loma Park, the hillside he made his own. Maybeck himself will tell you about each building, and at the women's gymnasium Julia Morgan joins him. Their words were written for this guide from the historical record, and their voices are dramatizations. Where a story runs past their lifetimes, I'll add a word at the end.

We begin at the Town and Gown Club, at Dwight Way and Dana Street.

## outro

That's the end of Walk 08, at the Temple of Wings, the house Maybeck began and walked away from. You're at the top of La Loma Park, among the houses he built for his friends, his clients and his family. Walk 10 explores more of this hilltop.
