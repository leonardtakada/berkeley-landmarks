## guide

Look around for a fraternity house from 1922, and you won't find it. The building that stood here is gone.

## hays

William Hays. I taught architecture at the university for nearly forty years, and I designed buildings for it too. In 1922 Clarence Dakin, whose father built the Studio Building downtown, and I designed a chapter house here for Kappa Sigma, in the period-revival manner the fraternities liked: a substantial house that looked as if it had always stood on Piedmont Avenue.

## guide

In 1992 the city made the Kappa Sigma house a landmark. A month later, on August 6, it was demolished to make way for the Haas School of Business, which stands here now. It's a reminder that a landmark designation alone doesn't save a building.
