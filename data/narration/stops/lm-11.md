## howard

John Galen Howard. This was my own house. I built it in 1912 on the corner of Le Roy Avenue and Rose Street, and we called it Rose Le Roy.

By then I had been the university's supervising architect for eleven years. Down the hill I was building on the grandest scale I could manage: the Mining Building, the Library, the Campanile, Wheeler Hall. Up here I wanted the same classical order, on a scale a family could live in.

In 1927 Julia Morgan added a library wing. Years before, I had engaged her to help supervise the Mining Building and the Greek Theatre. I knew the house was in good hands.
