## guide

The Elmwood Theater opened in 1914 as the Strand, a neighborhood cinema of about 740 seats. In 1946 Alexander Aimwell Cantin and Alexander Mackenzie Cantin remodeled it, and soon after it took the neighborhood's name.

It has been the Elmwood ever since. After a fire that started next door in October 1988, it was rebuilt with three screens. The Art Deco light fixtures inside were salvaged from the Merced Theatre, in the Central Valley town of Merced.
