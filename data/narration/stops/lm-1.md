## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. The Christian Scientists asked me for a church in 1910. Most of their churches were plain and classical, as rational as the faith. I wanted this one to feel like what happens inside it.

So I borrowed from everything I loved: a little Byzantine, a little Romanesque, Gothic tracery, and roofs that owe something to Japan. The plan is a Greek cross, with great crossed trusses over it, picked out in gold.

And I built it from ordinary things. Reinforced concrete. Industrial steel window sash, glazed with translucent glass. Panels of Transite, the asbestos board. Redwood for the trellises and the roof. Nothing precious, everything honestly used.

If the door is open, go in. The trusses are brown, the pillars gray, the tracery gold, with blue and red and green worked into the shadows.

## guide

When the university began buying up the blocks around it in the 1950s, the church's friends went looking for protection. In 1977 it was named a National Historic Landmark, the only one in Berkeley.
