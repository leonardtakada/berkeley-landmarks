## ratcliff

Walter Ratcliff. The Berkeley Tennis Club started here in 1906, with two courts of rolled earth. In 1908 my partner Alfred Jacobs and I built them a clubhouse beside the courts, in shingles, as woodsy as we could make it. The main room is paneled in unpainted redwood under a high-pitched ceiling, with a big fireplace of clinker brick.

The club caught on fast: five courts and more than a hundred and fifty members within a year. Hazel Hotchkiss joined in its first year and went on to win more than forty national championships. Later its juniors included Helen Wills and Helen Jacobs, who both played at Wimbledon.

In 1917 the club moved up the hill, below the Claremont Hotel, and this clubhouse became a home.
