## ratcliff

Walter Ratcliff. The oldest building here isn't mine. Channing Hall was designed in 1892 by Soulé Edgar Fisher for Miss Anna Head's school for girls, and it's one of the earliest shingled buildings still standing in Berkeley.

The school kept growing, and from 1911 into the late 1920s I kept adding to it. The last was Alumnae Hall, on the Haste Street side, finished in 1926. The girls needed one room big enough for the whole school: for morning assembly, which they called Chapel, and for dances, plays and concerts. It's shingled like the rest, with stenciling and color inside.

## guide

In 1963 the university took the campus by eminent domain, meaning to clear it for a parking structure. The garage was never built. Alumnae Hall, the last building put up here, was the first to be restored.
