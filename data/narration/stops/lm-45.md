## plachek

James Plachek. For a good many years I was Berkeley's city architect in all but name: libraries, fire stations, and schools like this one. I designed John Muir School in 1915.

The town was growing fast, and the children kept coming. A school, to my mind, should look like it matters, so I gave this one a classical front: a public building for the youngest public.

It's named for John Muir, the naturalist.

## guide

In 1974, John Muir and three other old schools were slated for demolition, with bond money set aside for seismic work. The newly formed Berkeley Architectural Heritage Association fought for them. Its very first walking tour ended with a reception in this school's auditorium.
