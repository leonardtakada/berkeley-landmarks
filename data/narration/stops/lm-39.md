## howard

John Galen Howard. A library is the center of a university, so I put this one at the center of the campus. Charles Franklin Doe, a San Francisco lumberman, left the university most of the money to build it, and it carries his name.

Go inside if you can, and climb to the great reading room on the north side. The ceiling is coffered. Massive Corinthian columns carry it, and stained glass lets in the light. I wanted students to feel that reading was a grand occupation.

## guide

The collections long ago outgrew Howard's building. In 1994 an underground addition, the Gardner Stacks, opened beside it.
