## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. The professors organized their club in 1902, and they wanted somewhere to talk, not somewhere to be impressive. So I built them something informal, in wood, among the trees by the creek.

Outside, the roof is low and gently pitched, which suits a Mediterranean climate. Inside the Great Hall, the ceiling is steep, like a Gothic hall, and I managed it without cross-beam trusses. Eight posts built up from rough two-by-tens carry the timbers, and the beam ends push out into the room.

Look at those beam ends. They're carved as dragon heads. My father was a master woodcarver, and they are for him.

There's a massive fireplace with a matte-glazed tile surround, and up in the clerestory windows, stained-glass shields of the colleges.
