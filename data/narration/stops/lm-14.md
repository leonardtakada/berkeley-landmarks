## guide

The Bancroft Hotel began in 1928 as the College Women's Club. Its architect, Walter Steilberg, trained under Julia Morgan, and you can see her school in it: a calm Mediterranean front, a great hall inside, balconies for the guest rooms and a terrace on the roof.

One room still carries his name. The fireplace in the Steilberg Room is faced with tile from the California Faience Company, which made its tile here in Berkeley.

The club rooms have since become a small hotel. Like the City Club a few blocks west, it began as a women's club.
