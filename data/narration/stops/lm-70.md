## guide

The Sigma Pi Chapter House, designed by Frederick Reimers in 1928 in the Mediterranean Revival style. It's now home to Phi Gamma Delta.

This is fraternity row. As the university grew in the early twentieth century, Greek-letter societies gathered along Piedmont Avenue, and many of the chapters built houses of their own. This one anchors the row.
