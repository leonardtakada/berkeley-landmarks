## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. Oscar Maurer was a photographer, one of the best of his day. Alfred Stieglitz himself singled him out. The earthquake and fire of 1906 took his studio in San Francisco, so in 1907, when he asked me for a new one here above the north fork of Strawberry Creek, I gave it stucco walls instead of shingles.

It's small, one story at the street. But look at how much is in it. At the entrance, a slender Corinthian column stands in a big plate-glass window. The beamed ceiling is stained red and blue. On the creek side the gables tumble down the bank like a village on a cliff, and at the back a tall leaded window sits under a broken pediment made of Spanish roof tiles.

Mediterranean, Mission, classical, Arts and Crafts, all at once. Three years later I did the same thing on a much larger scale, at the Christian Science church.
