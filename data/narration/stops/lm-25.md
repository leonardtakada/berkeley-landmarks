## thomas

John Hudson Thomas. I designed houses, mostly, and I liked to mix things that weren't supposed to go together: Arts and Crafts with the Vienna Secession, the medieval with the new.

Samuel Hume taught theater at the university, and in 1927 he wanted a house on this hilltop. People called it Hume Castle, or Hume Cloister. Look at the parapets along the roofline, cut into battlements like a fortress wall.

On a slope this steep, you don't fight the ground. You let the building climb it, and the house gets its view of the whole Bay.
