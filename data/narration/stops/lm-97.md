## guide

Spenger's Fresh Fish Grotto. In 1890 Johann Spenger, an immigrant from Bavaria, opened a clam stand on this spot. His son Frank turned it into a full restaurant in the 1930s, and his grandson, Frank Junior, known as Buddy, made it famous. By the 1950s the family claimed to be serving 3,500 pounds of fish a day.

In 1941 they added the Nautical Room, built with walls and floors salvaged from two ships, the Encinal and the Lurline, and a bar of koa wood from Hawaii.

Spenger's closed in October 2018, after 128 years on Fourth Street.
