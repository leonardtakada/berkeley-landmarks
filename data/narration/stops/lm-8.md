## guide

The Thorsen House, by the brothers Charles and Henry Greene. They designed it in 1908 for William Thorsen, a lumber baron, and his wife Caroline, and it is the only one of their "ultimate bungalows" in Northern California.

It was a showpiece of the Arts and Crafts movement and of the craftsmen who built it, with materials gathered from a global market. The woods came from the tropics. There is leaded art glass, and mother-of-pearl set into the woodwork.

It's now home to the Sigma Phi Society. When the city made it a landmark in 1975, the owners appealed. Today it's counted among the great architectural treasures of the nation.
