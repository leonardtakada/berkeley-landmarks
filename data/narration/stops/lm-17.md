## plachek

James Plachek. If you've walked around downtown, you've walked past my work: the annex behind the old City Hall, the Odd Fellows' Temple on Fulton Street, the Corder Building down at Bancroft. But this is the one I'd want you to see.

The library came to me in 1930. The new ornament then looked like the machine age, all zigzags and stepped lines, and I put it on a public building, where everyone who came in for a book would see it.

Take a minute with the facade. The geometry is the decoration.

## guide

Plachek's library has been the city's central library for nearly a century. It's a City of Berkeley Landmark and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.
