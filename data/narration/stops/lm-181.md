## morgan

Julia Morgan. This was the Baptist Divinity School, and I designed it in 1918 and 1919, here at the corner of Dwight Way and Hillegass Avenue.

I gave it what any school needs: good rooms, good light and honest materials. Large building or small, I tried to give each one the same care.
