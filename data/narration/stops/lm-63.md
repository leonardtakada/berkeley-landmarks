## howard

John Galen Howard. This hall carries the name of Benjamin Ide Wheeler, president of the university for twenty years. Wheeler was the one who recruited me, and the one who secured Phoebe Hearst's support for the plan of the campus.

It was built for teaching, classrooms for the humanities and social sciences, with a great auditorium inside, and it was finished in 1917 with state bonds. I gave it a long classical front of Ionic columns and a pedimented entrance, to hold the western edge of the campus core.

Planning it meant moving the front door of old South Hall, next door, from its west side around to the east.
