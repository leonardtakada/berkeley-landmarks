## guide

Bowles Hall, 1928, the university's first residence hall, and the first residence hall owned by the State of California. Mary McNear Bowles gave 350,000 dollars to build it, in memory of her husband, Phillip Bowles, a regent of the university.

George Kelham designed it in the Collegiate Gothic style, with a five-arched entry arcade, a crenelated tower and a tile roof, stepping down a steep hillside above the campus. It's reinforced concrete, and it sits right on top of the Hayward Fault.

For most of its life it housed only men. After a fourteen-month restoration, it reopened in 2016 as a residential college for students of every gender, with its common rooms, dining hall and library restored.
