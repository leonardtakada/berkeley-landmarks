## guide

The Manasse-Block Tanning Company. Tanners were working on this block in the 1890s. In 1898 a French immigrant, Prudent Remond, put up a three-story building here, promised as one of the largest leather-currying works on the coast. The complex's oldest building dates from that year.

In 1905 August Manasse and Roy Block, young men from the Bay Area's German-Jewish community, bought a tannery on this block and began to expand. Manasse came from a tanning family in Napa. Their company added buildings in 1905, 1906, 1922 and 1937, and made leather here until 1986, for the better part of a century.

The old tannery is now part of the Fourth Street shopping district.
