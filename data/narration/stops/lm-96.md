## guide

The Davis-Harmes House, a Victorian built around 1890 by C. W. Davis. It didn't start here. It stood at 733 Hearst Avenue until the 1960s, when building pressure threatened it, and it was moved to Fifth Street to save it.

It's an early example of moving a building to save it, a strategy Berkeley has used since to keep other old buildings standing. The move was a sign of the growing preservation movement that would eventually bring the city its landmarks ordinance.
