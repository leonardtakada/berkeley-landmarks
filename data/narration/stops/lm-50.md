## morgan

Julia Morgan. William Colby was a young mountaineer, and the Sierra Club was his life. He joined in 1898, became its secretary in 1900, and started the club's outings, the annual High Trips into the mountains, the next year.

In 1905 he asked me for a house here, on the corner of Channing and Warring. I was only a year or so into my own practice. I gave him brown shingles, plain and well made, the kind of house that belongs among trees.

## guide

Colby served as the Sierra Club's secretary until 1946, with two years out as its president, and led the High Trips until 1929. His work was central to the club's campaigns for Yosemite. The house is also known as the Blossom House.
