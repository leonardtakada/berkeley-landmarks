## howard

John Galen Howard. This stadium is a war memorial, built "in thankful memory of those Californians who in the War of Nations gave their all that we might live." I designed it in 1921, and it was finished in November 1923, just in time for the Big Game against Stanford. We won, nine to nothing.

President Barrows liked to say its dimensions slightly exceeded the Coliseum in Rome. Look at the rim, with its colonnade, the way a Roman arena should look.

I'll be honest. This canyon was not my choice. I wanted a site down nearer the town, where Edwards Field is now. But the trustees pressed for Strawberry Canyon, the university already owned part of it, and so we culverted the creek and dynamited Charter Hill for fill. Several of my fellow architects signed a letter against it. The Hayward Fault runs right underneath.

## guide

The stadium was finally given a major seismic retrofit, completed in 2012.
