## guide

The Veterans Memorial Building, by Henry H. Meyers, finished in 1928.

It was built for the people who had served: meeting halls and offices for veterans' organizations, with room for the city's community events as well.

Meyers kept to the classical manners of his neighbors, and that was the idea of a civic center. City Hall, the Post Office and this hall stand together around the park, each one formal and none of them showing off. The group is listed on the National Register of Historic Places as the Civic Center Historic District, and this building is one of its parts.
