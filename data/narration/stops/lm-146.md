## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. By 1931 I had been building on this hill for a quarter of a century, and I was nearly seventy. This house for the Tufts is made with old, ordinary ways of building, the kind any good carpenter knows, put together in ways they hadn't quite seen before.

On ground this steep, you don't fight the hill. You use it.

## guide

The city made it a landmark in 2002, as part of La Loma Park's cluster of Maybeck houses.
