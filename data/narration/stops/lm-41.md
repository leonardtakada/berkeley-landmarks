## guide

Founders Rock. College lore says that on April 16, 1860, the trustees of the College of California stood on this rock to dedicate the land they had just bought for a campus.

The story goes on. In 1866, still looking for a name for the new town, the trustee Frederick Billings is said to have recalled a line by the philosopher George Berkeley, Bishop of Cloyne: "Westward the course of empire takes its way." The town took the bishop's name, and so did the university the college became.

The class of 1896 set the plaque on the rock on Charter Day.
