## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. In 1914 the Kennedys wanted a studio for music here, at the corner of Euclid and Buena Vista: a room for teaching and playing. I gave them a hall framed in exposed timber. Wood is good to music, and I came back to the building again in 1923.

## guide

The building later passed to the Nixon family. As the Maybeck Recital Hall, it has been a home for chamber music for generations, and its acoustics are prized by the musicians who play here.
