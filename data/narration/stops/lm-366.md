## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. Gertrude Mathewson was the librarian at Berkeley High School. She brought me a Japanese plate and said, "Please match a house to it." I've never had a clearer brief.

So: a small house, compact inside, with broad low roofs and deep overhanging eaves, board-and-batten walls stained green, and doors and windows of many small panes. Look for the butterfly roof over the bay.

It stands across Buena Vista Way from where our own house was, on land that had been ours. Every other house I built on our land came after the fire of 1923. This is the one that was here before it.
