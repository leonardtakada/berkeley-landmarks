## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. The fire of 1923 burned our house to the ground. Afterward I wanted to build with something that wouldn't burn, and that didn't cost much. My friend John Rice had invented a material he called Bubblestone: glue, water and formalin whipped into a stiff foam and mixed with cement and sand. You dip burlap sacks in it and hang them on the walls like shingles. No skilled labor needed.

First I built a one-room house for my son Wallen, on the old foundation. Then Annie and I bought an abandoned real estate office, had it trucked up the hill and set on our old tennis court, clad it in Bubblestone and kept adding to it. We called it the Cottage, and from then on it was our home.

## guide

Maybeck was still living in the Cottage when he died, in 1957.
