## howard

John Galen Howard. California Hall was among the first buildings I put up under the Hearst plan, and it set the pattern for the rest. A steel frame, sheathed in reinforced concrete, and faced in granite: classical on the outside, modern underneath.

The state paid for it with an appropriation of 250,000 dollars, and it opened in August 1905. It held the university's administration, and the departments of history, political economy and commerce.

Stand back and compare it with the buildings I added later: the Library, the Mining Building, Wheeler Hall. They share the same granite and the same classical order. The plan was meant to make a single campus of them.

## guide

Since 1968 California Hall has housed the Chancellor's office.
