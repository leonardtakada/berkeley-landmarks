## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. Charles Keeler was my first client and my friend: a poet, a naturalist, a man who knew the birds of California by heart. Around 1902 he needed somewhere to work, so I built him this studio beside the house I'd done for him seven years before.

Keeler put what we believed about houses and how to live in them into a book, The Simple Home, in 1904. The Hillside Club took it as a manifesto.

## guide

The house and this studio are all that survive of the Maybeck cluster that once lined Highland Place.
