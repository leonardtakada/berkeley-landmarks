## guide

Finnish Hall. Its Finnish name is Toverii Tuppa, but most people just call it Finn Hall.

West Berkeley drew immigrants from all over the world, and the Finns were among them. They built this hall themselves between 1906 and 1909, with August Trille as the architect of record. It was their social and political home.

It's a plain building, beautifully crafted, and it has gone on hosting the town's gatherings. The Berkeley Architectural Heritage Association has met here more than once.
