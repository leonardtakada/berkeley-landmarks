## howard

John Galen Howard. Phoebe Hearst built this in memory of her husband, George Hearst, a miner who made his fortune in the ground and went on to the United States Senate.

We began in 1902 and finished in 1907. Samuel Christy, the dean of mining, worked with me on it, and Julia Morgan helped me supervise the work.

The heart of it is the memorial hall inside the entrance: three stories high, roofed with vaults of Guastavino tile. If the doors are open, step in and look up.

## guide

From 1998 to 2003 the building was retrofitted on base isolators, which let the ground move up to a meter sideways beneath it in an earthquake.
