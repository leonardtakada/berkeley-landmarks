## ratcliff

Walter Ratcliff. In 1908 a group of Berkeley women started the Berkeley Day Nursery, to look after the children of working mothers here in West Berkeley. It was the first nursery of its kind in California.

By the 1920s they had outgrown their old place on Tenth Street, and in 1927 I designed them this one. I didn't take a fee. Some work you do because it's worth doing.

I kept it to the scale of a house, in the Spanish Colonial manner, so a small child would walk into something that felt like home rather than an institution.

## guide

The playground and the old service wing are gone, but the building was carefully restored and has gone on serving the neighborhood, most recently as a family medical practice.
