## guide

The Silva House, built around 1870 by Joseph Alphonso. It is one of the very oldest buildings left in Berkeley. When it went up, Berkeley wasn't yet incorporated as a town. This was Ocean View, the first settlement, on the shore.

Its neighbor, the Davis-Harmes House, was moved here in the 1960s. This one has stood on Fifth Street from the start.
