## guide

The West Berkeley Macaroni Factory. After the 1906 earthquake, many Italian families from San Francisco settled in West Berkeley for good. Simone Marengo, a businessman from the Piedmont region of Italy who had lived here since the early 1890s, saw his chance: the newcomers had to eat. On an empty lot on Fifth Street, he built a pasta factory.

It opened in April 1907, two stories with a false front, fifteen skilled workmen and the latest machines. Marengo told the papers it was selling five thousand pounds a day: macaroni, spaghetti, vermicelli and more.

He was better at starting businesses than running them, and within a year his partner, Peter Costamagna, was in charge. Marengo became known as West Berkeley's unofficial mayor.
