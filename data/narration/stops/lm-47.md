## morgan

Julia Morgan. By 1940 I had been in practice for thirty-six years, and fashions had changed more than once. For the Fred Turner Building, here on Bancroft Way, I worked in the streamlined modern manner of the day.

People know me for houses, churches and clubs, and for a castle down the coast. But a working architect takes on shops and offices too, and I never thought a commercial building deserved less care than a church.

## guide

It is one of Morgan's later works in Berkeley. The city made it a landmark in 1981.
