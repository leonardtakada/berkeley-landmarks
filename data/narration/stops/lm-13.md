## guide

Fox Court, built in stages between 1927 and 1930. It looks like a page from a book of fairy tales, but behind the shops on University Avenue there are eighteen apartments, arranged around a planted courtyard.

People call the style Storybook, or Hansel and Gretel, or Mother Goose. At heart it's Tudor Revival, played for charm. Look at the walls: bricks of different colors, sizes and textures, laid unevenly, with rough stones pushed in among them.

It was designed by Carl Fox, who grew up in Grass Valley, studied mining engineering at the university, and worked as a mining engineer and a salesman in Asia before he and his brothers went into building. The Fox Brothers built cottages all over Berkeley from 1924 until the early 1950s, and their office was always on University Avenue.
