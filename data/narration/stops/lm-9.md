## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. Rose Walk is a staircase, a garden and a shortcut, all at once.

In those days the people up here walked down every morning to catch the streetcar on Euclid Avenue. The water company owned this slope by its reservoir, and it leased the land to the city and the Hillside Club for a dollar a year. The city engineer drew half a dozen plans for a path. Nobody liked any of them. So the Hillside Club raised the money from the neighbors, and I gave them the design for nothing: concrete steps, bordered with hedges of roses and trees.

It was finished in July 1913. Take a look at the double stair and the way it curves.

Ten years later the great fire burned the houses all around it. Henry Gutterson built the cottages you see along the walk, between 1923 and 1936, and he did them beautifully.
