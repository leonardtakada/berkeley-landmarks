## guide

The Cooper Woodworking Building, designed by Walter Crapo and built in 1912. It's also known as the American Photoplayer Company building, and player-piano mechanisms were made here.

It's a plain industrial building, and that's the point. In the early twentieth century this part of town was full of small factories and workshops making all sorts of things. This one survives, adapted to new uses rather than torn down.
