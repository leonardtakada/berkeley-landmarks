## plachek

James Plachek. In 1921 I was asked for a building to fill this whole block of Shattuck Avenue, from Bancroft Way to Durant. I kept the front classical and even, so a block-long building would read as one calm piece of street.

It has had more names than I can keep track of: the Corder Building, the Shattuck Apartments, the Whitecotton Building, the Witter Building. Downtown buildings change owners and names. A good front outlasts them all.

If you've been following my work, you'll find more of it nearby: the Central Library on Kittredge Street, and the old Federal Land Bank on the park by City Hall.
