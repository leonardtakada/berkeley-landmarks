## guide

The Arthur Ayers House, a Queen Anne Victorian from 1899. Nobody recorded its architect.

It went up as the streetcar lines were pushing out into this part of Berkeley, and builders followed the tracks with houses for sale. This one is well preserved, a good example of the houses built along the new lines.
