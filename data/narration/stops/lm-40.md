## howard

John Galen Howard. Jane Sather gave this gate in memory of her husband, Peder Sather, who had been a trustee of the old College of California. It was the formal entrance to the campus from the south, with a bridge over Strawberry Creek.

Look up at the panels. Professor Earl Cummings sculpted eight of them, figures standing for the disciplines: law, letters, medicine, mining, agriculture, architecture, art and electricity. The figures were nude, as figures in classical art generally are. Mrs. Sather did not approve, and down they came almost as soon as they went up.

## guide

The panels stayed down for more than sixty years. They were put back in the late 1970s. The gate now stands between Sproul Plaza and the creek, in the middle of the campus rather than at its edge.
