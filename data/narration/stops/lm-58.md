## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. By 1932 the Depression had set in. Carpenters were glad of fifty cents an hour, and I wanted to keep my office going. So we built two houses, nearly alike: one above Buena Vista Way for our son Wallen and his family, and this one, below the street, for Annie and me and our daughter Kerna.

By then I was building in stucco, with steel windows and knotty pine inside. Plain materials. But in my son's house across the way, the living room rises like a cathedral, and the outside is full of architectural jokes, down to a balcony hung on chains like a drawbridge.

## guide

The house carries two names: Kerna's, and her mother Annie's. It was among Maybeck's last houses. He was past seventy when it was built.
