## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. Years ago I designed Hearst Hall for Phoebe Hearst, a hall she gave the university as a gymnasium for its women. It burned in 1922, three years after she died. Her son, William Randolph Hearst, wanted a new gymnasium in her memory.

While John Galen Howard was away in Europe, the Regents gave the commission to Julia Morgan and me together.

## morgan

Julia Morgan. Mr. Maybeck was my first teacher. When I was an engineering student here, the university had no school of architecture, so a few of us studied with him in his own house. I was the only woman in the class. It was Mr. Maybeck who told me to go to Paris and try for the École des Beaux-Arts. While I was there, I helped him with the drawings for Hearst Hall.

So this building closed a circle. It's concrete, with classical detail and a tile roof in the Mediterranean way, and when it was finished in 1927 it was among the finest gymnasiums for women anywhere in the country.
