## morgan

Julia Morgan. In 1927 the women of Berkeley decided they needed a club for all of them, not one circle or another. They bought a lot running clear through from Durant Avenue to Bancroft Way, they raised every dollar themselves, and they asked me to design it.

I gave them a tower of six stories in reinforced concrete, with lower wings on either side that don't quite match, and I built it around two garden courts so that light and air reach the middle of the building. The windows open. They're framed in steel, and some are set in diamonds, like old leaded glass.

The members cared most about the swimming pool: seventy-five feet long, tiled, and opening through arches onto a walled garden.

I drew the light fixtures and the furniture too, and the dishes, and the linens. When the doors opened in 1930, the club had four thousand members.

## guide

It is a California Historical Landmark, and people still call it the Little Castle.
