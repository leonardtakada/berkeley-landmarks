## howard

John Galen Howard. This was the first building I designed for the university. Students had been using this hollow since 1894 for their gatherings, a rough bowl they called Ben Weed's Amphitheater. President Wheeler wanted something worthy of it, and William Randolph Hearst paid for it.

My model was the ancient theater at Epidaurus, in Greece. The seats rise in a half circle against the hill, and eighty-five hundred people can sit here.

I had help. Julia Morgan, a Berkeley graduate, worked with me on it.

It opened in 1903, and Theodore Roosevelt was among the first to speak from this stage.

## guide

The Greek Theatre has since heard the Dalai Lama and Desmond Tutu, and a century of concerts.
