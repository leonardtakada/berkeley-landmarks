## maybeck

Bernard Maybeck. Andrew Lawson was a geologist, the man who first mapped the San Andreas fault. In 1907, the year after the great earthquake, he wanted a house that would stand up to earthquakes and to fire. So I built him a villa in reinforced concrete, something like a house in Pompeii.

Concrete is a gray thing, so I tinted the stucco and scratched patterns into it while it was wet, the way the Viennese were doing. There are pergolas, and a curved balcony on the south side with an iron railing.

That same year I built my own house, up at Buena Vista and La Loma, in redwood shingles. In 1923 the fire came over the hill. Lawson's house came through it. Mine burned to the ground.
