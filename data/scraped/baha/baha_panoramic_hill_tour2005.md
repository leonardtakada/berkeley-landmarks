Panoramic Hill
Sunday, 1 May 2005, One to Five o’clock
A Photo Album

Mosswood Lane (photo: Daniella Thompson, 2005)
Guidebook in hand, approximately 1,500 tour goers climbed the leafy paths and stairs of Panoramic Hill during BAHA’s 30th annual Spring House Tour on 1 May 2005. Their exertions were rewarded by perfect weather, stupendous vistas, lovely gardens in full bloom, and vintage homes in a great variety of architectural styles.
