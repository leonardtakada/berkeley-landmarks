BAHA :: Ratcliff 100 House Tour Photo Album

The Residential Work of Walter H. Ratcliff, Jr.
in Claremont Park
Sunday, 7 May 2006, One to Five o’clock
A Photo Album

Montgomery House (photo: Daniella Thompson, 2006)
Nature smiled on 7 May 2006, as over a thousand tour goers converged on Claremont Park during BAHA’s 31th annual Spring House Tour. This year’s tour celebrated the residential work of Walter H. Ratcliff Jr. on the centennial of the architectural firm he founded. A large contingent of Ratcliffs was in attendance.
