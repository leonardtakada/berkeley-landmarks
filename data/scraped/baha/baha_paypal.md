BAHA :: How to use PayPal

How to use PayPal

To make a payment, click the Visa/MC or PayPal icon button directly below this line:

Before you go to PayPal, read these instructions:
Ignore Quantity. It is always fixed at 1, since the BAHA website has no shopping cart. You can purchase multiple items in a single order.
If you’re paying for a membership, indicate whether it’s new or a renewal. If you’re making a donation, we thank you.
After you’ve clicked the PayPal button, follow the example in the screen image below.
In the Description field, enter the purpose of the payment (e.g., the name of the event and the number of tickets you wish to purchase, or the title[s] of the book[s] you wish to order).

Add up all the ticket (or book) prices and enter the total amount in the Price per item box (no dollar sign). If you’re buying a book, add appropriate sales tax and shipping (see bookstore).

Click Continue, then follow PayPal’s instructions.

An automatic service charge will be added to each PayPal transaction.
It’s called a shipping charge but is, in fact, intended to cover PayPal’s service charge. Please do not deduct it from the amount you are paying.
