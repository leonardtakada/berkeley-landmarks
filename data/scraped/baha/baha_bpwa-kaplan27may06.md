BAHA :: Path Wanderers Architectural Tour of North Berkeley

Path Wanderers Architectural Tour
of North Berkeley
Saturday, 27 May 2006
Photos by Daniella Thompson
Alan Kaplan, retired East Bay Regional Parks naturalist and storyteller extraordinaire, led a Berkeley Path Wanderers walk focusing on some of North Berkeley’s architectural gems. Fifty to sixty people showed up.
