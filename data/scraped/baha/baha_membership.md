BAHA :: Support Preservation with your BAHA membership

Support BAHA
with your membership

Your annual tax-deductible membership dues include
a subscription to BAHA’s newsletter,
free research privileges in the BAHA archives, and
announcements of all BAHA activities.

Members in all categories have the right to cast one vote per election.
Household memberships have the right to cast two votes per election.

All members receive a discount on all publications bought through BAHA.
Additional benefits are outlined in the table below.

BAHA also offers a Student/Low-Income membership at $15 per year.
