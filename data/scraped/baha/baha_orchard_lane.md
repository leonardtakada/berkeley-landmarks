Berkeley Landmarks :: Orchard Lane

Orchard Lane
Panoramic Hill, Berkeley, CA
Daniella Thompson

Take a stroll down the picturesque Orchard Lane steps, designed by Henry Atkins and constructed in 1909–10. Orchard Lane was designated a City of Berkeley Landmark on 6 January 1992 (photos: Daniella Thompson, February 2004).
